The problem

Making plans should not feel awkward.

People want to be active and social. The planning is the hard part — scattered group chats, flaky maybes, no sense of the vibe, and showing up alone.

01

No more group chat chaos.

Join an activity and get the right chat automatically. One thread per game, class, or league.

02

No more guessing the vibe.

See the level, time, location, spots, and host before joining. You know what you're walking into.

03

No more showing up unsure.

The group is already in the thread. Questions answered, plans pinned, no walking in cold.

04

No more plans that disappear.

Recurring classes, weekly leagues, and saved hosts mean the next plan is always one tap away.

Four pillars

One app, four moves.

Each one supports the next. None of them are the end on their own.

01
Discover
Find nearby sports, fitness classes, clubs, leagues, pickup games, and local activities — sorted by your area and interests.
02
Join
Pick plans that match your level, schedule, and location. One tap to get in.
03
Connect
Activity-based chats help people coordinate before and after the plan. No more side DMs.
04
Share
Post photos, clips, and moments that show the active side of your life.
Chats

Every plan gets the right conversation.

When you join, you're in. Questions, updates, reminders, and coordination stay tied to the plan — not scattered across three group chats.

  • 01

    Pickup chats for players.

    One thread for the game. Open until the session ends.

  • 02

    Class chats for participants.

    Multi-session classes keep messages tied to the right session.

  • 03

    League chats and team chats.

    League-wide updates plus your team's own thread.

Community

Build a social life around what you actually do.

Profiles built around interests and skill levels. Posts and clips from real sessions. Connection through active identity — not random swiping.

  • Show interests, skill levels, and participation history
  • Share photos and clips from actual activities
  • Follow hosts, classes, and leagues you love
  • Connect through what you do, not what you scroll
